## Icon libraries and visual systems
- Lucide: https://lucide.dev/ — outline geometry and consistent stroke language.
- Tabler Icons: https://tabler.io/icons — functional icon families and stroke-based symbols.
- Phosphor: https://phosphoricons.com/ — weight variants and filled/duotone families.

## Platform guidance
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons
- Android adaptive icons: https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/launch/icon_design_adaptive
- Gemini image generation: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation

## How to use references
Study grids, optical weight, abstraction, negative space, family consistency and current platform requirements. Do not copy protected marks or assets without appropriate rights. When an existing licensed icon is a better fit than generating a new one, prefer reuse and comply with its license.
