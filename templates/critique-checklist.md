# Icon QA checklist

Mark each item **pass / fail / not tested** and add evidence where possible.

- Semantic clarity: metaphor matches function; neighboring icons are distinguishable.
- Silhouette: recognizable at actual display size; no unnecessary detail.
- Optical alignment: balanced weight, padding and centering.
- Family consistency: matching strokes, corners, palette, perspective, material and lighting.
- Accessibility: meaningful labels supplied by UI; not dependent on color alone; contrast checked in intended context.
- Raster: dimensions, alpha transparency, edge artifacts, clipping and accidental text checked.
- SVG: valid XML, correct viewBox, editable geometry, no scripts/external assets/raster elements; actual rendering checked.
- Platform: current launcher requirements and masks tested where applicable.
- Originality: no accidental imitation of protected marks or confusingly similar logos.
- Delivery: filenames, variants, formats and unresolved issues listed.
